        <h2>3. Chemistry does not guarantee a service life</h2>
        <p>
          Cycle-life figures describe a particular cell or pack under a stated
          test. Chemistry alone does not establish usable capacity, cycle life,
          calendar life, cold-weather performance, or replacement timing. Read
          the exact product documentation and compare the conditions behind
          each rating.
        </p>

        <h3>Lithium iron phosphate (LFP)</h3>
        <p>
          A cycle count is meaningful only with its test conditions and
          end-of-life definition. Preger et al. tested commercial lithium-ion
          cells under different cycling conditions and found substantial
          variation in the time and cycle count to 80% capacity. That study
          describes its tested cells; it is not a universal curve for every LFP
          product. See
          <a href="https://www.osti.gov/biblio/1650174">the study record</a>.
        </p>
        <p>
          For a product comparison, check the maker's stated charge and
          discharge limits, temperature, rate, depth of discharge, and capacity
          threshold. Do not convert a lab cycle count directly into a calendar
          service-life promise.
        </p>

        <h3>Sodium-ion</h3>
        <p>
          Do not infer a pack's usable range, voltage limits, storage behavior,
          cold-weather limits, or inverter compatibility from the chemistry
          name. Follow the exact battery manufacturer's operating limits,
          battery-management-system cutoffs, and the inverter maker's
          compatibility guidance. Treat any cycle-life figure as specific to
          the product and test conditions that produced it.
        </p>

        <h3>Lead-acid and AGM</h3>
        <p>
          Cycle life also depends on the particular model and its use. For
          example, Discover's AGM performance sheet says cyclic life depends
          on capacity taken, discharge rate, temperature, and charging method.
          Its cycle-life chart gives the test current, final voltage, charge
          current, and temperature. Those results describe the listed test, not
          every AGM battery. See the
          <a href="https://assets.discoverbattery.com/documents/07_sla/885_product_literature/standard-agm-battery-performance.pdf">manufacturer's performance sheet</a>.
        </p>
        <p>
          Across chemistries, use the exact model's warranty and maintenance
          instructions, and plan around the loads, environment, and service
          conditions you expect. A cycle count alone cannot establish how many
          years a home battery will last.
        </p>
