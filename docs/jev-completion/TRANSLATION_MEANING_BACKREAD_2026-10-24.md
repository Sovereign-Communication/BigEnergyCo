- de uses "Panels"/"Paneele" where the rest of the dictionary says "Module", and "amberfarbene" where elsewhere "bernsteinfarbene"; both are readable German-in-use, so they are terminology drift, not unreadable strings.
- nl uses the anglicism "engineer" where another string says "ingenieur"; fr mixes straight and typographic apostrophes; socTier/socCut strings mix " - " and "—" separators; `footerHeatmap` nl "Wereldkaart" drops "heat" but is internally consistent; `frontierTitle` ar "مالك" is ambiguous alone but resolved by context; the sizing engine is named differently across ar strings ("محرك التقدير" / "محرك الحساب") and de strings ("Dimensionierungs-Engine" / "Auslegungssoftware").
- Cross-locale label phrasing for the precise-location control varies ("precise/exact" vs "current"); each locale is internally consistent after fix #9.
