# Assumption register

Adopted 2026-11-05 under LANE PROTOCOL point 8 (R5 of the 2026-11-04
dissemination directive). Every load-bearing assumption behind this lane's plan
lives here as a row: the assumption, its owner, the date by which it must be
re-read, how it is verified, and the read that last checked it.

A row past its `verify-by` date is a **blocker** reported in the lane status
until it is re-read or retired. A row is not closed by having been written; it is
closed by a read of the thing asserted.

Status values: `VERIFIED` (read attached, current), `CARRIED` (last read is older
than the session reporting it, verify-by still in the future), `OVERDUE`
(verify-by passed, becomes a blocker).
