# Master Procurement & Educational Consulting Agreement (MPECA)

**THIS MASTER PROCUREMENT AND EDUCATIONAL CONSULTING AGREEMENT** (the "Agreement") is entered into by and between:

**BigEnergyCo LLC** ("Consultant" or "Company"), an educational hardware advisory and procurement consulting firm,  
AND  
**Client Name / Entity** ("Client" or "Buyer of Record").

---

### RECITALS
WHEREAS, Consultant provides open-source hardware reference material, direct-from-factory procurement coordination, and educational build mentorship for energy storage equipment;  
WHEREAS, Client desires to retain Consultant purely for non-goods educational advisory and purchasing agency services, while Client retains sole ownership, title, and installation responsibility as the Buyer of Record and Importer of Record;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

---

### 1. PURE CONSULTING SERVICES (NON-GOODS CONTRACT)
1.1 **Scope of Work**: Consultant shall provide non-goods educational advice, system sizing recommendations, component specifications, and limited purchasing agency coordination for factory-direct lithium iron phosphate (LFP) or sodium-ion (Na-Ion) battery hardware.  
1.2 **No UCC Article 2 Applicability**: Client explicitly agrees that this transaction is a fee-for-service consulting agreement and NOT a sale of goods under Article 2 of the Uniform Commercial Code (UCC). Consultant does not manufacture, stock, or re-sell physical hardware.  
1.3 **0-Touch Physical Protocol**: Client acknowledges that Consultant staff do not perform electrical contracting, licensed engineering stamps, or physical installation. Client acts as Lead Builder and System Owner.

---

### 2. PROCUREMENT AGENCY & IMPORTER OF RECORD STATUS
2.1 **Limited Purchasing Agency**: Client authorizes Consultant to transmit equipment specifications and purchase orders directly to Chinese factory vendors (e.g., EVE, JK BMS, Docan Power, GobelPower) on Client's behalf under Schedule A (LPAAA).  
2.2 **Importer of Record (IOR)**: Client is explicitly designated as the sole Buyer of Record and Importer of Record (IOR) for all customs, UN3480 hazmat declarations, duties, and Section 301 tariffs. Title and risk of loss pass directly from the overseas manufacturer to Client upon shipment release.  
2.3 **Pass-Through Hardware Cost**: Client pays factory hardware costs directly or passes through funds at 0% hardware markup by Consultant.

---

### 3. HAWAII ON-SITE CO-BUILD MENTORSHIP & PRICING
3.1 **Fee Structure**:
   * **Full Systems ($\ge \$5,000$ Procurement Fee)**: Systems with a $5,000+ procurement consulting fee (such as the standard 100kWh $10,000 system) include optional on-site in-person assembly guidance in Hawaii at **$0 extra charge**.
   * **Custom Systems ($< \$5,000$ Procurement Fee)**: Billed at a flat rate of **$50.00 / hour** for optional on-site build assistance.
3.2 **On-Site Role**: When Consultant provides on-site assistance at Client's property, Consultant acts strictly as an **educational mentor**. Client remains the Lead Builder and retains sole responsibility for property safety and local electrical compliance.

---

### 4. DISCLAIMER OF WARRANTIES & LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
4.1 **NO WARRANTIES**: ALL CONSULTING ADVICE, CAD BLUEPRINTS, WIRING DIAGRAMS, AND SOURCING RECOMMENDATIONS ARE PROVIDED "AS-IS". CONSULTANT EXPRESSLY DISCLAIMS ALL WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING MERCHANTABILITY AND FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.  
4.2 **THIRD-PARTY HARDWARE DEFECTS**: Consultant is not liable for manufacturing defects, cell degradation, voltage imbalance, or BMS failures caused by third-party overseas vendors.  
4.3 **LIMITATION OF LIABILITY**: Consultant's total aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall under no circumstances exceed the total net advisory fees actually received by Consultant from Client (capped at maximum $500.00 to $1,500.00 depending on contract terms).

---

### 5. ASSUMPTION OF RISK & INDEMNIFICATION
5.1 **Assumption of Risk**: Client understands and assumes 100% of the inherent risks associated with high-voltage DC electricity, lithium battery chemistry, cell thermal runaway, toxic off-gassing, arc flash, and local building code compliance.
