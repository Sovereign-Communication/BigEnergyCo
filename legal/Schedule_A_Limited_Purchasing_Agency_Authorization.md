# Schedule A: Limited Purchasing Agency Authorization Agreement (LPAAA)

**THIS LIMITED PURCHASING AGENCY AUTHORIZATION** is executed by the undersigned Client pursuant to the Master Procurement & Educational Consulting Agreement (MPECA) with **BigEnergyCo LLC**.

---

### 1. AUTHORIZATION OF PURCHASING AGENCY
Client hereby appoints BigEnergyCo LLC ("Agent") as Client's limited agent for the sole purpose of:
1. Negotiating volume pricing for Grade-A LFP/Na-Ion prismatic cells, BMS hardware, fuses, busbars, and compression frames directly with overseas manufacturers (Docan Power, GobelPower, EVE Energy, JK BMS).
2. Transmitting Client's order specifications and shipping address to factory freight forwarders.

---

### 2. IMPORTER OF RECORD & HARDWARE PASS-THROUGH
2.1 **Buyer & Importer Designation**: Client is designated as the sole Buyer of Record and Importer of Record (IOR) for all customs entries (CBP Form 7501), ISF filings, UN3480 HazMat declarations, ocean freight, and Section 301 tariffs.  
2.2 **Zero Hardware Markup**: Agent charges **0% markup** on physical hardware. All factory hardware costs ($4,980.00 for standard 100.48 kWh BOM) are passed through 1:1 to Client.  
2.3 **Direct Shipment**: Hardware is delivered DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) or CIF directly to Client's specified property address in Hawaii or worldwide.

---

### 3. FACTORY DISPUTES & WARRANTY CLAIMS
3.1 Agent agrees to assist Client in communicating with Chinese vendors for cell capacity or voltage delta claims.  
3.2 Client acknowledges that final cell replacement or refund decisions rest solely with the overseas cell manufacturer.
