        <h2>6. Build a cost comparison from stated inputs</h2>
        <p>
          Lifetime-cost comparisons depend on the exact products, their usable
          energy, installed price, warranty, operating conditions, and
          replacement assumptions. A cycle count alone does not establish a
          replacement date or total cost.
        </p>
        <ul>
          <li>State the battery model and its rated and usable capacity.</li>
          <li>Show the installed-price source, date, shipping, and tax.</li>
          <li>Describe the service conditions and manufacturer warranty.</li>
          <li>Make replacement, labor, recycling, and discount assumptions visible.</li>
          <li>Use the same cost boundary and analysis period for each option.</li>
        </ul>
        <p>
          Mark unavailable prices or service-life inputs as assumptions. Do not
          present a scenario as a guaranteed saving or a universal battery
          replacement schedule.
        </p>
