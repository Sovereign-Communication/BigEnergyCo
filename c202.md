### What was actually wrong: three guards with no test

`setSimpleMode`, `persistWizard` and the country-partition cache all write to storage behind `try/catch`, so Safari private mode or a `QuotaExceededError` cannot break the flow. All three guards are **correct**, and this PR does not change them. But **none had a test**, which is the car-1 shape: a guard nobody exercises is a guard a refactor deletes. Worse, the storage doubles in this suite are, by construction, the one kind of storage that never throws — so the guard could not fail even if it were removed.

Four tests now use a storage that throws on every operation and assert behaviour, not just absence of a throw:

- `setSimpleMode(true, throwing)` — no throw, state still flips, observers still notified, and the write was genuinely attempted under `beco-simple-mode-v2`;
- `persistWizard(wizard, throwing)` — no throw, the wizard still advances in memory, and `restoreWizard(throwing)` is a fresh wizard, not a partial one;
- `loadCountryCities("AL", fetch, throwing)` — the partition still loads when it cannot be cached;
- `cachedCountryCities("AL", throwing)` — `[]`, not a crash.

Each was verified to fail with its guard stripped and pass with it restored. The failure signature is the real one: `QuotaExceededError: quota exceeded` escaping into the toggle, the wizard and the city search.
