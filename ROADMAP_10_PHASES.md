# BigEnergyCo: 10-phase product roadmap

## North star

Help an ordinary household understand the cheapest, safest, most practical path to lower electricity bills and greater energy independence. The site is an independent decision engine, not a sales funnel.

## Product rules

- Bill reduction is the default entry point; off-grid remains a first-class path.
- 25%, 50%, 80%, 95%, and full off-grid are scenarios, never guarantees.
- Always separate bill savings, energy reduction, backup capability, and independence.
- Show the cheapest useful first step before the biggest system.
- Calculations come from deterministic code; AI explains and contextualizes them.
- Every result shows assumptions, confidence, constraints, and professional-review warnings.
- No accounts, lead capture, hidden ranking, or feature unlocks through donations.

## Phases

1. **Bill-first front door** — quick estimate from bill, location, and goal; clear paths for bill cutting, backup, and off-grid.
2. **Home energy map** — appliance builder, load categories, largest loads, flexible loads, and an “I don’t know” profile.
3. **Bill-cut explorer** — compare 25%, 50%, 80%, and 95% scenarios with cost, payback, imports, exports, and backup capability. — *shipped 2026-08-29 as the plausibility frontier: the full spend-vs-coverage curve rather than fixed steps. See `PHASE3_PLAN.md`.*
4. **Best first dollar** — rank efficiency, load shifting, solar, battery, generator, and other actions by cost, savings, resilience, and complexity.
5. **Realistic grid economics** — fixed charges, time-of-use rates, export compensation, demand charges, rate changes, and solar-only versus storage cases.
6. **Staged energy plan** — convert results into measure, reduce, generate, add resilience, and expand-independence steps.
7. **Reality check and safety** — identify shading, climate, electrical, permitting, cost, maintenance, and human constraints beside the result.
8. **Global and offline support** — local units, currency, grid standards, climate profiles, low-bandwidth operation, and offline calculations.
9. **Community validation** — opt-in anonymized projects, predicted-versus-actual performance, translations, climate profiles, and code references.
10. **Personal energy workspace** — local-first projects, monitoring imports, adaptive recommendations, outage mode, and grounded AI coaching.

## Polish phases 11–13

11. **Comprehension polish** — consistent labels, plain-language explanations, progressive disclosure, clear scenario hierarchy, and mobile-first result scanning.
12. **Trust polish** — visible confidence and data freshness, “why this result?” explanations, reproducible assumptions, honest edge cases, and no unsupported precision.
13. **Quality polish** — accessibility, keyboard and screen-reader coverage, offline/error states, cross-browser testing, performance budgets, copy consistency, and regression tests for every calculation contract.

## Success measures

- A new visitor gets a useful first answer in under two minutes.
- Users can explain why a battery is or is not recommended.
- Users compare at least two scenarios before choosing.
- The site identifies when 50%+ is plausible, fragile, or uneconomic.
- Actual user-reported performance can be compared with predictions.
- No scenario quietly turns an estimate into a guarantee.
