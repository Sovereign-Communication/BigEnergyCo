What this site does — and does not do

It does: estimate off-grid sizes (kW PV, usable kWh storage, kW inverter), grid-tie bill-cut targets (60/80/95%), battery-chemistry lifetime comparisons, and component-cost ranges with dated scopes — deterministically, free, no account.

It does not: sell hardware, take a cut, issue stamped designs or quotes, certify safety, or promise savings. Output is automated educational estimates and may be wrong. Chat text is sent to the AI provider to generate a reply and is not stored here; do not type anything sensitive into it.

Method: how numbers are produced

  • Five years of hourly NASA POWER weather at your exact coordinates — not one average sun-hours figure.
  • Worst-week sizing: arrays and banks are driven by the darkest week of five years, not the annual mean.
  • Explicit derates: temperature, soiling, wiring, and inverter losses as stated factors.
  • Tier A / B / C autonomy: from common cloudy spells through worst-week cover, each with explicit kW and kWh.
  • Grid-tie targets: 60%, 80%, 95% bill reductions against your load timing.
  • Deterministic engine: same inputs always produce the same sizes. The AI explains; it does not compute.

Pricing honesty: scopes, ranges, dates

  • Ex-factory (Aug 2026): $45–70/kWh batteries, $0.11–0.20/W panels — before freight, duty, BMS, labor.
  • Landed DIY: $80–125/kWh LFP ($88–138 sodium-ion), $0.16–0.28/W panels — shipped and duty-paid, you assemble.
  • Budget retail (Aug 2026): $110–165/kWh LFP ($110–175 sodium-ion), $0.28–0.38/W panels — shipped retail.

Results run ex-factory low through retail high with regional freight multipliers applied openly. All figures exclude mounting, wiring, permits, and labor — check current local prices.
