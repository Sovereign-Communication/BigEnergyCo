For orientation: landed DIY costs (cells + BMS + fusing + rack + freight + duty) typically land around $95–125/kWh, and cells alone DDP around $60–70/kWh — destination drives most of the spread. The retail figure is a published list price used as a reference point, not a quote, and a packaged product includes certification, warranty, and installer support that self-sourced parts do not. Common Questions

Short answers to what people ask most.

How accurate are the estimates?

Every number is a dated, scope-labeled range built from published market prices — not an AI guessing. Real-world results still vary with freight, duty, and what you can actually buy locally, so have a licensed professional verify anything you build.

Will a system pay for itself?

Where grid power is expensive — Hawaii, islands, much of Europe, Africa, South Asia — properly built solar plus storage commonly repays its component cost in one to four years, then runs nearly free for decades. The comparison above shows the hardware spread honestly.

What does a system actually cost?

Battery banks self-built from prismatic cells typically land around $80-125 per usable kWh shipped and duty-paid; budget retail packs run roughly $110-165. Panels add about $0.16-0.38 per watt. Every result states its price scope honestly.

Do I need a generator?

Only if you want near-total independence through weeks of bad weather. Sizing tools show exactly that trade-off: one system never needs a generator, one needs it about a day a year, one only now and then. Grid-connected users skip this entirely.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. This page is fully offline by design — it works on Freenet and any connection-free environment. The cost math runs entirely in your browser and nothing is fetched from the network.

Is building my own battery safe?

It demands respect: lithium cells store enough energy to start fires, and DC arcs don't switch off. The guides here insist on proper fusing, BMS use, and code compliance — and on having a licensed electrician check anything before you energize it.
