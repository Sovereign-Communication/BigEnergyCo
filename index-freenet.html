What a ~100 kWh DIY Build Is Made Of

A reference parts list so you know what the components are and roughly what they cost. Prices are indicative figures from the open market as of August 2026 and change constantly. Nothing here is sold by this site.

Cells

EVE MB31 3.2V 314Ah Cells

Brand new Grade-A prismatic LFP cells. 16S configuration per pack (51.2V nominal). QR codes verified clean.

~$43.50 / cell (indicative, Aug 2026)
BMS

JK 200A Active Balance BMS

Smart active balancing (2A balance current), dual temperature probes, RS485/CANbus comms with Sol-Ark & Victron.

~$92.00 / unit (indicative, Aug 2026)
Fusing

Eaton Class-T 200A Fuses

20kA AIC interrupt rating @ 160V DC. Trips short circuit overcurrent in <2ms before BMS MOSFET stress.

~$14.50 / unit (indicative, Aug 2026)
Rack & Compression

300 kgf Compression Racks

Uniform Belleville spring washer compression endplates. Maintains 3,000 N pressure to double cycle life.

~$28.00 / rack (indicative, Aug 2026)
