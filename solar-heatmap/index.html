      How this works & The Generator Paradox

      Why do fragile grids look slow on paper, but solar is actually exploding there? In countries like Nigeria (43% unelectrified, remaining 57% on rolling blackouts), Lebanon, Haiti, and Pakistan, official utility rates look low ($0.05–$0.09/kWh) because power is heavily rationed or subsidized. When the power cuts out, families and businesses burn petrol and diesel generators at $0.40–$0.65/kWh.

      • Real-World Basis (Default): Blends official rates with generator fuel costs weighted by unserved population and average grid outage duration. Nigeria's true payback drops from 8+ years to under 1.5 years.
      • Grid Only Basis: Assumes a 24/7 unlimited stable grid at brochure utility prices.
      • Solar yield: Annual kWh per kW of panels simulated from NASA satellite weather stations.
      • System cost: Panels + LFP battery + inverter at landed-DIY pricing including regional freight.
