Escape Load Shedding With Solar: Backup Sizes (2026)

📅 Sep 7, 2026 · Lucas Ballek · 11 min read · every number below comes from this site's deterministic simulator, not guesswork

The short answer: most blackout pain is 2–3 kWh/day of essentials — fridge, lights, phones, wifi, fan — and that is small: roughly 0.8–1.5 kW of panels with 2–4 kWh storage. The full home at 8–12 kWh/day needs roughly triple. Start from loads, build in stages, size on the worst week.

South Africa, Nigeria, Lebanon: different cuts, same structure

South Africa: staged blocks 2–12 hrs/day with notice. Nigeria: 4–12 hrs grid/day, unpredictable rotation of grid, generator, solar. Lebanon: 0–4 hrs state power plus diesel subscription capped by amps. Common structure: cuts of 2–6 hours, 1–3x daily, hitting morning and evening peaks — exactly what an essentials battery covers best.

Start from loads, not headlines

  • Fridge modern: 80–200 W × 8–12 hrs → 0.8–1.6 kWh/day.
  • LED lights: 5–10 W × 5–8 × 4–6 hrs → 0.1–0.5 kWh/day.
  • Phones + laptops + wifi: 0.2–0.6 kWh/day family.
  • Fan: 40–75 W × 6–10 hrs → 0.3–0.8 kWh/day.
  • TV LED: 40–120 W × 2–5 hrs → 0.1–0.6 kWh/day.

Typical essentials total: 2–3 kWh/day, evening peak 0.3–0.8 kW. Peak sets inverter; energy sets battery.

Essentials vs full home

Cooking (1–2 kW), geysers (2–4 kW), pumps (0.75–2.2 kW with 3–7x surge), AC (0.8–2 kW) each exceed the entire essentials peak. Full-home backup costs 2.5–4x the essentials system. Most successful builds start essentials, prove one shedding season, then expand with measured data.

Staged build path

  • Stage 1 — silent backup: inverter + battery with grid charging. Covers 2–6 hr blocks immediately.
  • Stage 2 — bill-cutting: add 1–3 kW panels, target 60–80% cut.
  • Stage 3 — off-grid tiers: expand toward Tier A/B/C autonomy.

Build 48 V from the start with inverter headroom so stages reuse, not replace. Grid-tied export and mains changes need licensed review and certification.

Why worst-week sizing matters

Average sun misleads backup design. This engine sizes against five years of hourly NASA POWER weather and reports the worst week as design driver, with Tier A/B/C selecting how much you ride through. Ask any vendor which cloudy spell their number survives.

Batteries and inverters for backup duty

Daily shedding is cycling duty: LFP (~6,000 cycles) and sodium-ion handle it for years; lead-acid at 50% usable needs double nominal plus replacements at $240–380/kWh usable. Low-frequency hybrids with 2–3x surge ($85–260/kW) start fridges where high-frequency units trip. Automatic changeover (10–20 ms) keeps wifi alive.

Frequently asked

What size solar for load shedding?

Essentials 2–3 kWh/day: 0.8–1.5 kW + 2–4 kWh + 1–2 kW inverter. Full home 8–12 kWh/day: 3–5 kW + 10–16 kWh + 3–5 kW inverter.

Can I start small and expand?

Yes: battery first, then 1–3 kW panels, then storage toward Tier A/B/C. Choose 48 V with headroom from the start.

Will grid-tied solar work during cuts?

No without hybrid plus battery and changeover — standard inverters shut down for anti-islanding safety.

How long will a battery last in a blackout?

Usable kWh ÷ load kW: 4 kWh on 0.5 kW ≈ 8 hrs; on 2 kW ≈ 2 hrs, minus inverter draw and derates.
