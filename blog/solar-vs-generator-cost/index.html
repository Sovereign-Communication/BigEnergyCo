Solar vs Generator Running Cost: Honest Fuel Math (2026)

📅 Sep 7, 2026 · Lucas Ballek · 11 min read · every number below comes from this site's deterministic simulator, not guesswork

The short answer: a small generator delivers power at $0.30–$0.80/kWh in fuel and oil alone at $1–2/L, while solar-plus-battery levelizes to roughly $0.08–$0.22/kWh over 10–15 years (Aug 2026 component ranges). The generator is cheaper to acquire; solar is usually cheaper to run.

Generator fuel math: litres to $/kWh

$/kWh = fuel price ($/L) ÷ efficiency (kWh/L). Small 2–7 kVA units make roughly 2.5–3.5 kWh per litre:

Fuel price 2.5 kWh/L 3.0 kWh/L 3.5 kWh/L
$1.00/L $0.40/kWh $0.33/kWh $0.29/kWh
$1.50/L $0.60/kWh $0.50/kWh $0.43/kWh
$2.00/L $0.80/kWh $0.67/kWh $0.57/kWh

What fuel math misses

  • Oil and filters every 100–200 hrs: $0.01–0.06/kWh.
  • Plugs, valves, overhauls: $0.03–0.12/kWh over life.
  • Part-load penalty: a 5 kVA unit at 0.5 kW night load can double or triple $/kWh that hour.

Fuel-plus-consumables commonly land $0.30–$0.90/kWh served for small daily-run units.

Noise, fumes, fuel logistics

  • 65–85 dB at 7 m — night running strains neighbours.
  • Exhaust must never enter living spaces (CO risk).
  • 10 kWh/day at 3.0 kWh/L burns 3–4 L/day, 100–120 L/month of jerrycans.

Worked example: 10 kWh/day in Lagos and Beirut

Lagos: at 3.0 kWh/L and $1.00–1.50/L, fuel alone is $1,200–1,825/year for 3,650 kWh. Solar-plus-storage (3.0–4.5 kW panels, 10–14 kWh storage, $2,200–6,000 by scope) commonly pays back in 1–4 years, then marginal kWh are nearly free.

Beirut: diesel subscription $0.30–0.60/kWh plus $120–700/year fixed fees = $1,100–2,900/year. Rooftop 3–4 kW plus 8–12 kWh bank shifts surplus into cut hours; $2,000–5,500 by scope. The estimator sizes on the worst week, not the average.

When the generator still wins

  • Rare outages (20–100 hrs/yr) — fuel never repays panels.
  • Dark-week cover — small generator cheaper than doubling bank.
  • Surge-heavy workshop loads.
  • Rental / mobile / temporary sites.
  • Hybrid optimum: solar for 90–97% of kWh, generator for the rest.

Frequently asked

How much does it cost to run a generator per kWh?

$0.29–0.80 fuel plus $0.04–0.18 consumables at $1–2/L. Your logbook litres ÷ kWh is ground truth.

Is solar cheaper than running a generator?

Daily-run yes ($0.08–0.28 vs $0.30–0.90). Rare-outage no. Hours run per year decides.

Can solar fully replace a generator?

Often in sunny regions; dark-week cases favour a small retained generator as hybrid backup.

Should I keep my generator after installing solar?

Often yes, as backup charger via proper changeover with licensed review. Never backfeed improvised connections.
