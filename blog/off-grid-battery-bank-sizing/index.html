Battery Bank Sizing Calculator: Off-Grid Guide (2026)

📅 Sep 7, 2026 · Lucas Ballek · 10 min read · every number below comes from this site's deterministic simulator, not guesswork

The short answer: size usable kilowatt-hours first, then convert to amp-hours at your voltage, then derate for chemistry. A 10 kWh/day home often tests 12–20 kWh usable (250–420 Ah at 48 V) for Tier B coverage. Educational estimates only — verify with a licensed professional.

Usable vs nominal: DoD decides what you get

  • LFP lithium: plan ~90% usable.
  • Sodium-ion: similar usable window.
  • Lead-acid / AGM: plan ~50% usable.

Nominal needed = usable ÷ DoD. Example: 15 kWh usable needs 16–18 kWh nominal LFP, but 28–32 kWh nominal lead-acid.

Series vs parallel math

  • Series adds voltage, Ah unchanged.
  • Parallel adds amp-hours, voltage unchanged.
  • Energy: kWh = Volts × Ah ÷ 1000.

Example: four 12 V 280–320 Ah LFP in series = 48 V at 280–320 Ah (13–15 kWh nominal, 12–14 kWh usable). Two such strings in parallel = 48 V at 560–640 Ah (26–30 kWh nominal).

12 V vs 24 V vs 48 V

  • 12 V: cabins 0.5–1.5 kWh/day, peaks under 1 kW.
  • 24 V: 1–4 kWh/day, peaks 1–2 kW.
  • 48 V: homes above 3–4 kWh/day or peaks above 2 kW. Lower current, easier expansion.

Current check: 3,000 W ÷ 12 V = ~250 A. 3,000 W ÷ 48 V = ~60–65 A. That division is why nearly every 5–10 kWh/day home ends up 48 V.

Inverter class comes from peak kW

  • Peak 0–2 kW: test 2–3 kW inverter class.
  • Peak 2–4 kW: test 4–6 kW class.
  • Peak 4–8 kW: test 8–10 kW class.
  • Motors: allow 2–3x surge for seconds.

Worked 10 kWh/day example at 48 V

  • Daily load: ~10 kWh/day.
  • Autonomy 1.0–1.5 days → 10–15 kWh baseline.
  • Dark-week margin x1.5 → test 15–22 kWh usable Tier B.
  • Ah usable = kWh × 1000 ÷ 48: 12 kWh ≈ 250 Ah; 20 kWh ≈ 415 Ah.
  • Nominal: LFP ÷ 0.9, lead ÷ 0.5 (double).

Safety: fuse, breaker, wire, licensed pro

  • Fuse every parallel string plus main DC disconnect.
  • Class-T high-interrupt fusing on large LFP banks.
  • Torque, gauge, and lug quality matter as much as the diagram.
  • No article substitutes for licensed review before energizing.

Frequently asked

How do I calculate battery bank size in Ah?

Usable kWh × 1000 ÷ volts. 15 kWh at 48 V ≈ 310 Ah usable. Then ÷ 0.9 LFP (≈345 Ah nominal) or ÷ 0.5 lead (≈620 Ah).

How many batteries for 10 kWh/day off-grid?

12–20 kWh usable Tier B (250–420 Ah at 48 V); 25–40 kWh Tier A. Typically 1–2 matched 48 V 300 Ah strings Tier B.

Is 12 V, 24 V, or 48 V better?

48 V for full-time homes above 3–4 kWh/day. 12 V only for small cabins.

Do series or parallel increase capacity?

Series raises voltage; parallel raises Ah. kWh = V × Ah ÷ 1000 either way.
